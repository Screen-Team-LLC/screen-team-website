Lanai problems we fix every week

  • Door panel blows — hinge side and handle side are high-traffic failure points
  • Roof sag — weight and UV loosen horizontal runs
  • Tree punctures — oak and pine debris through roof panels
  • Oxidized mesh — gray, brittle screening that snaps when touched
  • Pop-outs — spline dried out and screening pulled free from the groove
Lanai door tip: If the door drags on the sill after mesh sagged, forcing it worsens the tear. Stop using it as a main exit until Chris re-tensions or replaces the door panel — usually a half-day fix.

Mesh choices for lanais

Standard fiberglass for most lanais. Pet-resistant on lower panels and doors where dogs wait to go out. No-see-um for homeowners near water who fight tiny biting insects standard mesh does not stop. Chris explains tradeoffs — visibility, airflow, and durability — without pushing the most expensive option.
