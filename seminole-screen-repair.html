When to patch vs rescreen in Seminole

If one or two panels tore in a storm and the remaining mesh is tight and white, patch. If the cage looks uniformly gray and brittle, Chris will recommend full rescreen — patching brittle mesh wastes money when the next panel fails weeks later. He explains that on the phone before you book.
