Pool cage repair Clearwater homeowners ask about

Most Clearwater pool cage repair calls start the same way: a storm tore the top horizontal row, a dog popped the lanai door spline, or the mesh turned chalky gray and failed in a line across the cage. Chris inspects the aluminum before quoting mesh — bent posts or corroded bases mean a structural conversation, not a hidden upsell inside a rescreen price. Partial repairs often run $175–$750; full rescreens commonly $2,500–$6,500+ depending on height and panel count.
