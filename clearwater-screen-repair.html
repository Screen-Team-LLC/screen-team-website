Clearwater weather on pool cages — coastal vs inland

Gulf-side cages along the beach corridor and Island Estates show white corrosion and chalky bottom rows first from salt spray. Chris replaces lower horizontals when upper mesh is still tight — see storm screen repair after severe weather. Inland Countryside lots fail more from UV oxidation and wind-driven rain on roof panels; uniform chalk that snaps in your fingers usually means a full rescreen is the honest long-term fix.

Afternoon squalls track east across Pinellas and peel roof horizontals before walls fail. Pet-resistant mesh is a frequent upgrade for Clearwater households with dogs that lean on lanai screening. If the latch will not catch but mesh looks fine, the door panel — not the roof — is often the first repair on beach-adjacent properties where hinge corrosion outpaces screen wear.
