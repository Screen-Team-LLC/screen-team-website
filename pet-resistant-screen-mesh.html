Pet mesh vs a full cage rescreen

Many households only need the door leaf and the first horizontal row upgraded. That keeps visibility and airflow on upper walls while hardening the zone pets actually hit. When the entire enclosure is chalky and brittle, mixing bright pet mesh with oxidized neighbors looks worse than a coordinated full pool cage rescreen with pet-resistant material specified on abuse panels. Chris walks that tradeoff on the phone so you are not guessing from a product catalog.
