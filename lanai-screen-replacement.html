Replacement is for when patching keeps failing

Lanai screen replacement is the right call when every wall has turned the same chalky gray, spline pulls free in multiple corners, or you are prepping a home for sale and mismatched patch panels show from the street. The Screen Team LLC removes old mesh systematically, inspects the aluminum track, and rolls new screen with even tension so the patio feels sealed again — not like a quilt of old repairs.

Not sure if you need replacement or a single-panel fix? Text photos to (727) 386-6562. Chris will tell you which side of the line you are on.

What changes during a lanai replacement

  • Mesh type — standard fiberglass, better visibility, or pet-resistant upgrades
  • Door rebuild — hinge-side spline and latch alignment included when the entry failed first
  • Roof panels — horizontal sections tensioned to shed afternoon rain
  • Debris removal — old spline and brittle mesh hauled off, not left on the lanai floor
Pinellas note: Many Seminole and Largo lanais from the 1990s have non-standard panel sizes — Chris cuts to fit rather than forcing stock mesh that gaps at corners.

Replacement vs lanai repair

Lanai repair handles one or two torn sections when the rest of the enclosure is still white and tight. Replacement makes sense when oxidation is uniform or you want one clean color before listing the house. Chris prices both paths honestly — he does not earn more by pushing a full refresh when a door panel would suffice.
