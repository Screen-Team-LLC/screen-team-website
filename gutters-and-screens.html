Why enclosure gutters are not house gutters

A pool cage roof sheds a sheet of water that standard five-inch house gutters were never sized to catch. That is why Chris points many cage homes toward super gutters — larger-capacity enclosure troughs built for screen roof runoff. If your cage is tight but every storm leaves a waterfall at the house connection, the mesh is not the villain. Signs gutters are damaging your screens

  • Dark streaks on top lanai panels directly below a gutter seam or overflow corner
  • Rust at cage attachments where water drips from a loose downspout onto aluminum
  • Sagging header — saturated fascia pulling the lanai frame out of square
  • Standing water on the patio after rain stops, right where roof panels meet the house
  • Oak and pine debris choking troughs above Pinellas and Pasco pool cages every summer
  • Repeat roof-row tears in the same bay after every storm cell — classic overflow fatigue
