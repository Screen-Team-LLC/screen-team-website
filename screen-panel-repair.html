Screen Panel Repair — Tampa Bay, FL

One torn section, one visit, one straight quote — Chris replaces individual panels on cages, lanais, porches, and windows.

Not every tear needs a full rescreen invoice

Screen panel repair is the smallest scope we offer — and the one homeowners put off until a second panel fails. The Screen Team LLC replaces isolated sections when surrounding mesh is still tight and white: a golf-ball hole in a porch panel, one pool cage roof horizontal after a squall, a lanai wall with a single puncture. Panels often start around $75 each; every completed job carries a $150 minimum charge even on quick fixes.

Text a photo to (727) 386-6562. Chris counts panels with you and flags when full rescreen math beats endless patches.

When panel repair stops making sense

If you are on panel six and the enclosure still looks gray, oxidation won out. Chris will recommend replacement or a full cage rescreen instead of charging you for a seventh patch. That honesty is why repeat customers in New Port Richey and Pinellas Park call the same number for the next house.
