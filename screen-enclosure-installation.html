Screen Enclosure Installation

From pool cages to patio enclosures, installation means structure — not just mesh. Chris Westcott helps Tampa Bay homeowners define the enclosure they want, then coordinates licensed partners when engineering and permits enter the picture.

Installation vs rescreen — different jobs

Screen enclosure installation creates or replaces the aluminum structure that holds the mesh. Rescreening refreshes fabric on a frame that already exists. Mixing those words online causes budget shock later. Screen Team starts every inquiry by sorting which job you actually need.

For true installations — new footprints, tear-downs, or major structural changes — Chris leads the customer relationship and scoping. Permit-heavy engineering is partner-coordinated. For sound existing frames, he steers you toward rescreen services instead of selling a rebuild you do not need.

Enclosure types we help plan

  • New pool cages over existing or new pools
  • Lanai and patio enclosures
  • Custom layouts with irregular footprints
  • Spa-inclusive designs with dedicated wings
  • Door packages and mesh specifications for Florida insects
Install tip: Decide drainage early. Enclosure roofs without a plan for super gutter installation often create fascia and patio water problems within a few seasons.

What stays with Screen Team

Scoping visits or photo reviews, option explanations, mesh education, and ongoing communication. You always know who to call. Partner trades handle stamped engineering and permit-required structural erection when those are mandatory for your project.

Materials, doors, and Florida wind context

Enclosure installation conversations cover more than pretty elevations. Door swing clearances, emergency egress habits, and how the roof sheds water onto fascia all affect long-term happiness. Chris documents those choices early so partner engineering — when required — reflects how your household actually moves through the yard.

Coastal Pinellas lots may need different corrosion awareness than inland Pasco pads. Hillsborough projects sometimes face HOA packet cycles that stretch calendars. None of that is a reason to skip planning; it is a reason to start with a scoping call that sorts install versus rescreen before anyone draws a full custom cage you do not need.

Bring survey notes, pool equipment locations, and any future spa or outdoor kitchen plans. Those details change post placement more than brochure photos admit. Screen Team keeps the conversation practical so you approve a path that fits budget and permitting reality in your city.
