New Lanai Enclosure

A new lanai enclosure turns a slab into usable Florida evenings. Chris Westcott helps you define size, doors, and mesh — and coordinates licensed partners when the job needs permits and engineering rather than a simple rescreen.

Lanai enclosures built around how you live

Some lanais are three-season sitting rooms; others are outdoor kitchens with pass-through traffic. New lanai enclosure planning should start with furniture layout, grill clearance, and where people actually walk — not a generic rectangle drawn on a napkin. Chris asks how you use the space so door swings and panel heights match real life.

If you already have a screened lanai with a tired frame that still stands true, lanai screen replacement or a lanai rescreen may be enough. New structure is for missing enclosures, failed frames, or expansions.

Partner-honest when permits apply

Many lanai builds still trigger local permit review — especially when attaching to the home, adding roof structure, or changing footprint. Screen Team leads scoping and customer communication. Permit-heavy engineering goes through licensed partners when required. You are never left wondering who is responsible for inspections.

  • Patio and lanai footprints tied to existing slabs or new concrete
  • Knee-wall vs full-screen looks
  • Sliding or hinged door packages
  • Mesh selection for bugs, pets, and view
  • Gutter and drip-edge planning at the house connection
Lanai tip: Measure the furniture you refuse to give up before locking width. Enclosures that look fine empty feel tight once the sectional arrives.

From open patio to usable evenings

New lanai enclosures succeed when airflow, shade, and bug control match how you entertain. Chris asks about televisions, ceiling fans, and grill exhaust so the screened volume does not fight your furniture layout. If an older screen room already exists with a tired but square frame, he will steer you to mesh replacement instead of a partner-engineered rebuild.

Pasco and Pinellas HOAs sometimes regulate visible frame colors and roof styles. Share those rules early. A clean scoping packet speeds partner drawings when permits apply and prevents redesign fees later. Call with slab dimensions and two wide photos from opposite corners of the patio.
