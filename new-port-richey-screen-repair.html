What fails first on Pasco pool cages

West Pasco sun is relentless on standard fiberglass mesh. Spline dries out and pulls away from the groove; dogs push lanai doors until the bottom edge pops free; tree limbs puncture roof panels after summer squalls. Chris inspects the aluminum frame before quoting: if posts are plumb and headers are solid, a rescreen restores years of life. If a post is bent or a footing has shifted, he tells you before any mesh goes up.
