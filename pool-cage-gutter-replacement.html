Pool Cage Gutter Replacement

When enclosure gutters are crushed, rusted, or simply too small, replacement beats another season of fascia stains. Chris Westcott replaces pool cage gutters with runs built for Tampa Bay storm volume.

Replacement when the old line is done

Pool cage gutter replacement is the clean reset: remove failed troughs, evaluate attachment points, and hang a system sized for the roof that feeds it. Many cages still wear the original undersized gutters from decades ago. Storm after storm, those runs overflow even when “clean.” Replacement is how you stop treating symptoms.

Chris frames replacement inside the super gutters service line. You get enclosure-aware sizing, not a generic house gutter dropped onto a cage header and hoped for the best.

Replacement vs repair decision points

  • Multiple open seams or end failures along one run
  • Deformed troughs that no longer hold shape
  • Chronic overflow after professional cleaning
  • Missing sections after storm impact
  • Desire to upsize while a full rescreen already needs ladder time
Replacement tip: If paint streaks on the house track every joint in the old gutter, the system is telling you it has leaked for years — section patches rarely silence that entire line.

What replacement day looks like

Pool cage gutter replacement begins with protecting plants and deck surfaces near the header, then removing the failed line without damaging sound enclosure members. Chris checks whether fasteners pulled through soft aluminum or wood trim that needs attention before new hangers go in. Ignoring substrate problems just recreates sag in a year.

New runs are hung with enclosure volume in mind — the same philosophy as our super gutters hub. Corners, end caps, and outlet drops are detailed so the first summer storm is a proof test, not a surprise. If lanai gutters on the same elevation are half-failed, replacing them in the same visit prevents a waterfall from jumping onto the new cage line.

Coastal homes should mention salt exposure; hardware and maintenance expectations shift slightly. Inland Tampa cages often fail from oak debris load more than salt. Either way, replacement is the reset when cleaning and caulk have already been tried.
