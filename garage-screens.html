Garage screens — airflow without the bug parade

Florida garages heat up fast with the door closed and become insect highways with the door open. A proper garage screen system lets you work, exercise, or park with the bay up while palmetto bugs and mosquitoes stay outside. The Screen Team quotes manual retractable systems from about $899 and motorized options from about $2,299 — sized to your opening.

Chris Westcott measures the opening, discusses how you use the garage, and recommends manual vs motorized based on daily habits — not a one-size sales pitch.

Systems we install and repair

  • Manual retractable screens — single and double-car bays
  • Motorized retractable systems — remote operation, higher openings
  • Mesh panel replacement on existing systems
  • Track and tension adjustments when screens bind
  • Heavy-duty mesh for high-traffic or workshop use
Garage screen tip: Measure clear width and height of the opening — not the door panel size. Chris confirms rough opening on site; garage door tracks and light fixtures affect fit.

Manual vs motorized — practical differences

Manual suits homeowners who raise the screen occasionally — weekend projects, seasonal ventilation. Motorized wins when the screen goes up and down daily or the opening is too wide for comfortable manual pull. Chris quotes both when you are undecided so you can compare installed price, not brochure fantasy.
